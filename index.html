01 / THE IDEA

A little more
out of everything.

x stands for anything. ext stands for extension. xext is a place to explore how far we can take what we already have.

That could mean adding a feature, modifying a device, improving a process, or making a small budget go further. Some experiments are practical. Some are playful. Every one is a chance to learn.
